## Words and patterns we do not use

Seed list. Add your own. Vesper's `ai-tell-check` and `mechanics-only` skills read this section.

- Em dashes. Use a comma, a period, or a conjunction.
- The construction "it's not X, it's Y".
- leverage, seamless, robust, cutting-edge, game-changing, transformative, unlock, empower, best-in-class, end-to-end
- "at the end of the day", "here's the thing", "the reality is", "in today's fast-paced world"
- Rule of three for its own sake. Two examples or four are fine when that is how many there are.
- Rhetorical questions that answer themselves in the next line.
